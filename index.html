How I lead

  1. Outcomes over output

    Teams own a problem and a metric, not a feature list. We measure success by what changes for customers and the business.

  2. Context, not control

    Clear strategy and guardrails, so product managers can make good calls quickly without waiting on me.

  3. Commercial fluency for every PM

    Product managers who understand pricing, pipeline and retention build better products and earn a stronger voice in the business.
