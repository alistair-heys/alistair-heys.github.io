## How posts work

Every post is a Markdown file in the `_posts` folder, named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`.
The bit at the top between the `---` lines is the front matter: a title and an optional one-line description.

Everything below is ordinary Markdown:

- **Bold**, *italics* and [links](https://alistairheys.com)
- Lists, headings and quotes

> Most B2B SaaS companies don't have a product problem. They have a commercial alignment problem.

Commit the file to GitHub and the post goes live in a minute or two.
